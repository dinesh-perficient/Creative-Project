## Draft A — The NFS Most Wanted Hook (Recommended)

The last time I felt this kind of momentum while learning something new was about 15 years ago, clearing blacklists in NFS Most Wanted. Every blacklist I cleared didn't just give me a checkmark — it unlocked a new part of Rockport city. New roads, new shortcuts, new connections between places I'd already been.

That's exactly how this learning path feels right now.

Last week, I completed "AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations" by Anthropic — my first step toward the Claude Certified AI Fundamentals (CCAF) certification. It introduced the 4D Framework: Delegation, Description, Discernment, and Diligence. Solid theory, but I didn't yet see how deep it went.

Today, I completed "Claude 101 by Anthropic" — and the map started filling in.

Claude 101 covers 18 topics across everything Claude can do — from basic prompting to Projects, Artifacts, Skills, Connectors, Research mode, and all the surfaces where Claude lives (web, terminal, Slack, browser, Microsoft 365, design tools). It's a lot of ground.

But here's what surprised me: the course didn't introduce new theory. Every single feature mapped back to the same 4D Framework.

- Struggling with generic responses? That's a Description gap — you didn't give enough context.
- Blindly trusting AI output? That's a Discernment gap — you need to evaluate, not just accept.
- Choosing the wrong tool for the job? That's a Delegation gap — Platform Awareness matters.
- Connecting AI to your data without thinking about permissions? That's a Diligence gap.

Projects are infrastructure for persistent Description. Skills are codified Description at the methodology level. Connectors are Delegation multipliers that raise the Diligence bar. Research mode is Delegation at its fullest expression, where Discernment becomes critical at the output level.

The 4D Framework isn't just one course's model. It's the operating system underneath everything.

And like Rockport city, every new topic I complete doesn't just add a pin on the map — it reveals roads connecting to places I've already been. The learning compounds. The map gets richer. And I can't stop exploring.

Two courses down. CCAF certification path continuing.

More to come as the map fills in.
