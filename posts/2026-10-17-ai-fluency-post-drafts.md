## Draft A — The Realization Story (Recommended)

Last week, I hit a wall.

I'd been relying heavily on AI tools to do the thinking for me — and when circumstances changed, my output quality took a hit. That was a wake-up call. The problem wasn't the tools. It was me.

So I went looking for what I was missing and found exactly the right course at the right time: "AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations" by Anthropic on LinkedIn Learning.

The core idea is simple but powerful: AI Fluency isn't about mastering a specific tool. It's about developing skills that work regardless of which AI you use.

The course introduces the 4D Framework:

- Delegation — Knowing what to do yourself vs. what to hand to AI
- Description — Communicating clearly so AI actually understands what you need
- Discernment — Evaluating AI output critically instead of accepting it blindly
- Diligence — Taking full ownership of what you deliver, AI-assisted or not

What hit me hardest was how directly I could map each of these to my own gaps. I wasn't giving AI enough context about the problem. I was accepting plans without questioning them. I was delegating the thinking, not just the execution.

The biggest takeaway: AI tools will keep changing. Budgets will shift. Models will update. But if you build fluency in how you *work with* AI, you're prepared for all of it.

This is Day 1 of my path toward the Claude Certified AI Fundamentals (CCAF) certification — and the beginning of something bigger.
