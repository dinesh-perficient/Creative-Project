A living knowledge portfolio documenting Dinesh's learning journey toward AI fluency and career transformation. Not a tool or application — a documentation and visualization project.

2. Visualize how learnings connect and form a path over time

His output quality dropped — not because the tools got worse, but because the powerful models had been compensating for gaps in his foundational AI skills. He discovered AI Fluency and started the CCAF path to build tool-agnostic skills.

## LinkedIn Post Guidelines

### Privacy (CRITICAL)
- NEVER expose project details, team details, org internals, team size, client information, or employer name
- Abstract all context: "a large-scale project" not specifics, "complex architecture" not tech stack details
- The insight is what matters, not the specifics that produced it

### Tone
- Frame challenges positively — "hiccups" not "mistakes", growth not failure
- Confident learner sharing insights, not someone confessing errors
- No self-deprecation
- Keep authenticity — the insight is real, just framed constructively

### Purpose
- Posts are a public learning log for Dinesh's future self, NOT optimized for engagement or followers
- Don't optimize for hooks, brevity, or audience attention spans
- Keep full detail and depth — it's documentation, not marketing
- The "see more" acts as a filter — whoever clicks actually cares
- Still natural and personal in voice — but don't sacrifice substance for reach

## Key Connections and Insights

- The 4D Framework (AI Foundations) is the foundation everything else builds on
- Every common Claude challenge traces back to a Description gap (except hallucination = Discernment)
