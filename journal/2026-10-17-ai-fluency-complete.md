## 1. Why AI Fluency?

AI Fluency is the practical skill set for interacting with AI systems in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe**. It's tool-agnostic — survives any model change, restriction, or update.

### Three Ways We Engage with AI

| Mode | What it means | Who drives |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Automation** | AI executes specific tasks based on your instructions | You instruct, AI executes |
| **Augmentation** | You and AI collaborate as thinking and execution partners | Shared, back-and-forth |
| **Agency** | AI works independently on your behalf; you configure its knowledge and behavior | You configure, AI operates |

The 4D competencies apply across all three modes.

---

## 2. The 4D Framework (Overview)

| Competency | Core question |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Delegation** | What work should I do vs. what should AI do? |
| **Description** | How do I communicate clearly with AI? |
| **Discernment** | Is what AI produced actually good? |
| **Diligence** | Am I using AI responsibly? |

These aren't sequential steps — they work together in a continuous loop.

## 3. How Generative AI Works (Foundation)

Modern generative AI (LLMs) exists because of three breakthroughs:
1. **Algorithmic/architectural** — the transformer architecture
2. **Data** — vast amounts of digital training data
3. **Compute** — dramatic increases in computational power

**Two-stage learning:**
- **Pre-training** — analyzing patterns across billions of examples
- **Fine-tuning** — learning to follow instructions and provide helpful responses

**Current capabilities:** Versatility across tasks, conversational awareness, connecting with external tools

**Current limitations:**
- Knowledge cutoff dates
- Hallucinations
- Context window constraints
- Challenges with complex reasoning

**Key principle:** The most effective applications combine human and AI strengths — humans provide critical thinking, judgment, creativity, and ethical oversight.

## 4. Delegation (Deep Dive)

Delegation is about making thoughtful decisions about work distribution. Not "automate everything" — create the most effective human-AI partnership.

### Three Components

**Problem Awareness** — Understand your goals and the nature of the work *before* involving AI.
- What am I trying to achieve?
- What does the work actually involve?

**Platform Awareness** — Know the capabilities and limitations of different AI systems.
- What can this AI do well?
- Where does it struggle?

**Task Delegation** — Strategically distribute work to leverage the strengths of each.
- What do I do best here?
- What does AI do best here?
- Where do we collaborate?

**Requires both:** domain expertise AND understanding of AI capabilities.

### My mapping (from experience)
I was failing at Problem Awareness and Platform Awareness. I threw a complex multi-system problem at AI without understanding either the problem deeply enough or the AI's limitations in navigating large codebases. Result: AI couldn't focus on the right files, and I couldn't tell that its approach was wrong.

## 5. Description (Deep Dive)

Description goes beyond writing prompts — it's creating a collaborative environment.

### Three Components

**Product Description** — Clearly define what you want.
- What output do you need?
- What format, audience, style?

**Process Description** — Guide how AI approaches the task.
- What steps should it follow?
- What method or reasoning should it use?
- Can be as important as specifying the end goal.

**Performance Description** — Define how AI should behave during collaboration.
- Concise or detailed?
- Challenging or supportive?
- What communication style works for this task?

**Key insight:** AI can't read your mind. The quality of results comes down to how clearly you articulate your needs, preferred approach, and desired interaction style.

## 6. Effective Prompting Techniques

### Six Foundational Techniques

1. **Give context** — Be specific about what you want, why you want it, and relevant background
2. **Show examples** — Demonstrate the output style or format you're looking for
3. **Specify constraints** — Clearly define format, length, and other requirements
4. **Break complex tasks into steps** — Guide AI through multi-step reasoning
5. **Ask AI to think first** — Give space for working through the process
6. **Define AI's role or tone** — Specify how you want AI to communicate

### Secret Weapon
Ask the AI itself to help improve your prompt.

### Principles
- Prompting is **iterative** — expect to refine based on results
- Successful patterns: clear task overviews, format specs, explicit constraints, relevant background

## 7. Discernment (Deep Dive)

Discernment is the flip side of Description. Description communicates your intentions; Discernment evaluates whether what you received meets your needs.

### Three Components

**Product Discernment** — Evaluate the quality of outputs.
- Is it accurate?
- Is it appropriate?
- Is it coherent and relevant?

**Process Discernment** — Assess how AI approached the task.
- Are there logical errors?
- Did it miss important considerations?
- Is the reasoning sound?

**Performance Discernment** — Evaluate AI's behavior during interaction.
- Was the communication style effective?
- Did it engage the way you needed?

### The Description-Discernment Loop

1. **Describe** what you need (Product, Process, Performance)
2. **Discern** what you receive (Product, Process, Performance)
3. **Refine** based on your discernment — feedback on what worked and what didn't
4. **Integrate** your own expertise — add your perspective, make final decisions, take responsibility

### My mapping (from experience)
I was blindly accepting AI-generated plans without evaluating whether the approach made sense for my specific architecture. No Process Discernment at all.

## 8. Diligence (Deep Dive)

Diligence addresses the ethical and safety dimensions. The other 3 Ds focus on effectiveness and efficiency; Diligence ensures the collaboration is also responsible.

### Three Components

**Creation Diligence** — Be thoughtful about which AI systems you use and how.
- Is this the right tool for this task?
- Am I using it appropriately?

**Transparency Diligence** — Be honest about AI's role in your work.
- Who needs to know AI was involved?
- What are the expectations in this context?

**Deployment Diligence** — Take ownership of AI-assisted outputs you share.
- Have I verified the output?
- Am I vouching for this by sharing it?

**Context matters:** Personal, academic, and professional contexts have different expectations for disclosure and verification.

### My mapping (from experience)
I am accountable for the delivery. Data safety is mine. Output quality is mine. AI helps — but the responsibility doesn't transfer.

## 9. Conclusion — Key Principles

- AI Fluency develops through **intentional practice**, not overnight mastery
- Our **expertise and judgment** remain the foundation — AI doesn't replace that
- The most powerful outcomes emerge when **humans and AI build on each other's strengths**
- AI systems are powerful but not magical — they're only as useful and safe as **we enable them to be**
- The framework is designed to **remain relevant** as AI evolves — that's why it's tool-agnostic
